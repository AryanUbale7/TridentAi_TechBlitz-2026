# CHIKITSA DESK - Complete User Guide

## Welcome to CHIKITSA DESK

CHIKITSA DESK is a comprehensive healthcare management platform designed to streamline appointment booking, medical records management, and healthcare interactions between patients and healthcare professionals.

---

## Getting Started

### Login Page
1. Navigate to the login page from the home page
2. Enter your email address
3. Enter any password (demo mode accepts any password)
4. Click "Login"

**Demo Accounts Available:**
- **Patient Account**: john.doe@email.com
- **Doctor Account**: dr.smith@smartclinic.com

### Creating a New Account
1. Click "Sign Up" on the home page
2. Select your role (Patient or Doctor)
3. Fill in your details:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Phone Number
   - Date of Birth (for patients)
   - Specialization (for doctors)
4. Create a password
5. Click "Create Account"

---

## Header Navigation & Account Management

### User Menu (Top Right)
The header contains your account menu in the top-right corner. Click on your profile information to open the dropdown menu.

### Account Switching
If you have multiple accounts registered in the system:
1. Click on your profile in the header
2. Look for "SWITCH ACCOUNT" section
3. Click on any other account to switch to it
4. You'll be redirected to the appropriate dashboard

### Logout
1. Click on your profile in the header
2. Click the red "Logout" button
3. You'll be redirected to the login page
4. Your session will be cleared

## Patient Features

### Dashboard
Access your personal dashboard showing:
- Quick action buttons (Book Appointment, Find Doctors, Medical Records, etc.)
- Appointment statistics (Total, Upcoming, Completed)
- Recent appointments list
- Easy navigation to all features

### Book Appointment
1. From your dashboard, click "Book Appointment"
2. Select a doctor from the available list
3. Choose a preferred date and time slot
4. Add any notes or concerns
5. Confirm the booking
6. You'll receive a confirmation

### Find Doctors
1. Go to "Find Doctors" section
2. Filter by:
   - Medical specialty
   - Experience level
   - Ratings and reviews
3. View doctor details including:
   - Professional experience
   - Patient ratings
   - Reviews from other patients
   - Available time slots

### Medical Records
Access your complete medical history:
- Past appointments and notes
- Medical diagnoses
- Lab tests and results
- Prescribed medications
- Health conditions

### Prescriptions
View all prescriptions:
- Current medications
- Dosage information
- Frequency of intake
- Duration
- Special instructions

### Doctor Reviews
1. Go to a specific doctor's profile
2. View existing reviews and ratings
3. Add your own review:
   - Give a star rating (1-5)
   - Write your feedback
   - Submit your review
4. Ratings help other patients choose doctors

### My Profile
- View and edit your personal information
- Update contact details
- Manage health information
- View account settings

### Appointments List
- View all your appointments (past and upcoming)
- Filter by status
- Cancel appointments if needed
- Reschedule appointments
- View appointment details

## Doctor Features

### Dashboard
Your professional dashboard showing:
- Quick action buttons (View Schedule, My Profile, etc.)
- Key statistics:
  - Total appointments
  - Scheduled appointments
  - Completed appointments
  - Your rating and reviews
- Recent appointments list

### Schedule Management
1. Go to "View Schedule"
2. See your weekly calendar
3. Manage your availability:
   - Set working hours
   - Block time slots
   - Mark unavailable days
4. View upcoming appointments
5. Prepare for appointments in advance

### Appointment Details
1. Click on any appointment
2. View patient information
3. View appointment notes
4. Mark appointment status:
   - Scheduled
   - In Progress
   - Completed
   - Cancelled
5. Add case notes
6. Create prescriptions

### Prescriptions
1. Open a patient's appointment
2. Create a new prescription:
   - List medications
   - Specify dosage
   - Frequency of intake
   - Duration
   - Special instructions
3. Patient will receive the prescription

### Case History
1. During or after an appointment
2. Document the case:
   - Symptoms reported
   - Diagnosis given
   - Treatment provided
   - Follow-up date
   - Professional notes
3. Case history is saved for medical records

### My Profile
- View your professional information
- Update qualifications
- Update specialization
- Manage bio and experience
- View your ratings and reviews

## Common Tasks

### How to Book an Appointment
1. Login to your patient account
2. Go to "Book Appointment" or "Find Doctors"
3. Select a doctor
4. Choose date and time
5. Add any health notes
6. Confirm booking

### How to View Your Appointments
1. Go to "Appointments" or "Dashboard"
2. See all your appointments
3. Click on any appointment for details
4. View appointment history

### How to Complete an Appointment (Doctor)
1. Open the appointment
2. Review patient information
3. Click "Complete Appointment"
4. Add case notes
5. Create prescriptions if needed
6. Save

### How to Leave a Review
1. Navigate to a doctor's profile
2. Scroll to reviews section
3. Click "Write Review"
4. Select star rating
5. Write your feedback
6. Submit review

### How to Switch Accounts
1. Click your profile in the header
2. Look for "SWITCH ACCOUNT"
3. Click another account
4. You'll switch to that account

### How to Logout
1. Click your profile in the header
2. Click "Logout" button
3. Confirm logout
4. You'll be redirected to login page

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Login?
- Verify your email is correct
- Check that your account is created
- Clear browser cache and try again

### Can't See an Appointment?
- Refresh the page
- Make sure you're logged into the correct account
- Check appointment status

### Doctor Not Showing Up?
- Check doctor's availability
- Try different dates
- Refresh the page

### Notification Not Appearing?
- Refresh the page
- Check notification settings
- Make sure you're logged in
